# Flashnear Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-04-22

This Privacy Policy describes how Flashnear ("Flashnear", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you use our mobile applications for iOS and Android (the "App"). By using the App you consent to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Contact
- Company: Flashnear
- Email: support@flashnear.com

If you have questions, requests to exercise your privacy rights, or concerns about this policy, contact us at support@flashnear.com.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information when you use the App:

- Account information
  - Email address obtained when you sign in with Google Login.
- Location information
  - Precise or approximate device location when you grant location permission. This enables location-based features in the App.

We do not collect other personal information unless you voluntarily provide it.

## 2. How We Collect Information

- Directly from you
  - When you sign in via Google Login.
  - When you contact support.
- Automatically
  - When you grant the App access to location services on your device.
- From third parties
  - From Google as part of Google Login and Google Maps integrations.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for these purposes:

- Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the App and its features (including location-based functionality).
- Authenticate and manage your account (via Google Login).
- Respond to support requests and communicate important account or product information.
- Personalize and enhance your experience in the App.
- Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

We will not use your email or location data for purposes materially incompatible with the purposes listed above without obtaining additional consent where required by law.

## 4. Third-Party Services and Sharing

We integrate with the following third-party services that may receive data from or about you:

- Google Login (Google OAuth)
  - Purpose: Authentication and basic account information (email, profile identifier).
  - What is shared: With your consent, Google provides us with your email address and basic profile details necessary to create and manage your account.
  - Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

- Google Maps
  - Purpose: Display maps and provide location-based features.
  - What is shared: Your device location (when you grant permission) may be transmitted to Google Maps APIs to render maps and location-based content.
  - Google Maps privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose personal information to service providers and partners who perform services on our behalf (for example, analytics and infrastructure providers) and only to the extent necessary for them to perform those services. We require such service providers to protect personal information consistent with this Policy.

## 5. Legal Basis for Processing (where applicable)

Where applicable (for example, for users in the EU), our lawful bases for processing personal information include:

- Consent: When you grant permissions (e.g., location) or sign in with Google.
- Performance of a contract: To provide the App services you request.
- Legitimate interests: To operate, secure, and improve the App, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights.

## 6. Data Retention

- Account email: Retained for as long as your account exists and thereafter for a reasonable period to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.
- Location data: Retained only as long as necessary to provide the location-based features you request, analyze and improve the App, or as required by law.

If you request deletion of your account, we will delete or anonymize personal information within a reasonable period except where retention is required by law.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights including:

- Access: Request a copy of personal information we hold about you.
- Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
- Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal exceptions).
- Restriction or objection: Object to certain processing, including for direct marketing or profiling.
- Data portability: Request a machine-readable copy of your data.
- Withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any rights, contact us at support@flashnear.com. We may request information to verify your identity before fulfilling requests. We will respond to valid requests in accordance with applicable law.

California residents: Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you may have the right to request disclosure and deletion of certain categories of personal information. To make a request, email support@flashnear.com. We will follow required verification procedures. We do not knowingly sell personal information of consumers.

## 8. Location Permission Controls

You control location permissions through your device settings:

- iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Flashnear → Choose "While Using the App", "Ask Next Time", or "Never".
- Android: Settings → Apps → Flashnear → Permissions → Location → Allow or Deny.

Revoking location permission may limit or disable location-based features in the App.

## 9. Google Account and OAuth Controls

To manage or revoke Google Login access:

- In the App: Use account or settings screens to sign out or disconnect your Google account (if such a feature is provided).
- Google account settings: Visit https://myaccount.google.com/permissions to review and revoke third-party app access granted to Flashnear.

## 10. Security

If we become aware of a security breach affecting personal information, we will take steps to contain the breach and, where required by law, notify affected users and regulatory authorities.

## 11. International Data Transfers

Flashnear may transfer personal information to countries outside your jurisdiction, including to the United States, for storage and processing. Where applicable, we will take steps required by law to ensure adequate protection for international transfers (for example, standard contractual clauses).

## 12. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for users who are at least 13 years old (and at least 14 where required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the applicable minimum age. If you believe we have collected personal information from a child in violation of this policy, contact us at support@flashnear.com and we will take steps to delete the information.
